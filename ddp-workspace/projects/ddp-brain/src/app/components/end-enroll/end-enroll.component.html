Enrollment is now CLOSED for the Brain Tumor Project (BTproject)





Thank you to everyone who has participated in the project. We continue to analyze and share the data generated through your partnership, which will help continue to shape the future of research in brain tumors.

Brain Tumor Project Timeline

    2019

  • Project launched through collaboration with patients, advocates, and caregivers, open to adults with primary brain tumors. At the time of launch, the project was named “The Brain Cancer Project.”

    2020

  • After additional engagement with patients and caregivers, the project is expanded to enroll pediatric patients. The website is redesigned to make it more inclusive to all ages

    2021

  • Continued feedback from the community leads to the project being renamed “The Brain Tumor Project.”

  • Project team engages in Brain Tumor Social Media (#BTSM) monthly Twitter chat about the project.

  • Partnership with Children’s Brain Tumor Network (CBTN) announced.

    2023

  • Yearly social media campaign during Brain Tumor Awareness Month (May) including participant testimonials and staff highlights.

  • Exhibited at the American Brain Tumor Association (ABTA) 50th Anniversary conference, American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) Special Cancer Conference on Brain Cancer, ABTA 5k in NTC, and University of Florida Cancer Chomp 5K.

  • Cohort of diffuse midline glioma (DMG) and atypical teratoid rhabdoid tumor (ATRT) samples collected through CBTN are molecularly characterized (sequenced) in order to expand the pediatric brain tumor data available to researchers.

    2025

  • Over 315 patients with primary brain tumors have fully enrolled. Data generated through the project will be de-identified and added to repositories for ongoing research.
